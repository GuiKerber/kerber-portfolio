Cross-Platform
Coaching Experience

Desktop redesign + mobile MVP for a remote fitness coaching platform, connecting trainers and students when in-person sessions were no longer possible.

Case structure

During COVID, personal trainers lost the ability to coach in person, putting both client retention and recurring revenue at risk. This project expanded an existing trainer platform into a connected product ecosystem: a redesigned desktop experience for coaches and a mobile MVP for students.

ClientTRYBE
Period2020–2021
RoleLead Product Designer
PlatformWeb · iOS · Android

The existing platform supported workout management, but not a complete remote coaching experience. As in-person sessions stopped, trainers began losing students, income, and continuity.

  • Help trainers continue delivering personalized coaching remotely
  • Give students easy access to workouts created by their own coaches
  • Modernize the desktop platform and align it with a stronger product experience

The work was structured in two parts. First, a design audit of the existing desktop platform, mapping friction points in workout planning, content organization, and client management.

  • Improved workout plan creation with a weekly organization
  • Created wireframes to define flows, hierarchy, and content requirements
  • Redesigned the desktop platform with a more consistent UI system
  • Designed a mobile MVP focused on workout access and guided training

The redesigned desktop platform gave coaches the tools to build and manage programs efficiently, with a clearer structure and a UI aligned with the new brand direction.

  • Improved workout plan creation and weekly organization
  • Clearer exercise library with search, filters, and categories
  • More efficient program assignment and client management flows
  • Updated UI aligned with the new brand direction

The mobile MVP was built for students, giving them a practical and motivating way to follow trainer-assigned workouts independently during remote sessions.

  • Onboarding and account creation
  • Access to trainer-assigned workouts
  • Guided exercise flow during training sessions
  • Visibility into workout progress and weekly results

The platform had been losing students as in-person sessions stopped. The redesign gave trainers a way to keep delivering remotely and gave students a way to train on their own, closing a loop that had broken.

Measured in internal testing
  • –40% time to build a workout plan A day-by-day setup flow replaced a single long form.
  • –30% time to assign a program to a student Assignment moved onto the program board, instead of routing through a separate client list.

Both measured as timed task comparisons against the previous desktop flow, before launch.

Defined to track after launch
  • Trainer retention — how many trainers kept using the platform after launch
  • New trainer signups — how many joined once remote coaching became possible

I designed the platform and defined what should be measured, but did not have access to product analytics after handoff.
