Checkout flow redesign for a fashion e-commerce with R$480M in annual GMV — driven by hypotheses, A/B experiments and behavioral analysis.

Case structure

CRO project for one of Brazil's largest fashion e-commerce sites. The brief was clear: increase checkout conversion without a full redesign, using data as a compass and A/B testing as the method.

CompanyFashion Retailer (NDA)
Duration4 months · 2023
RoleLead UX + CRO Strategist
Team2 designers, 1 analyst, 4 devs
GMVR$480M annually

The checkout had a 67% drop-off rate at the payment step. Heatmap analysis, recordings and GA4 data revealed 3 distinct behavioral patterns before abandonment.

  • 67% drop-off at the payment screen — above industry average (58%)
  • Users returned to the cart 2.3 times before abandoning
  • 38% of abandonments occurred when viewing the CVV field
  • Mobile had 2× more abandonment than desktop in the same flow

We built a hypothesis framework prioritized by potential impact × ease of implementation. 7 hypotheses were selected for simultaneous testing.

  • H1: Progress indicator reduces drop-off at the payment step
  • H2: Always-visible order summary increases trust
  • H3: Inline card entry (no modal) reduces CVV friction
  • H4: Social proof on the payment screen increases conversion

4 rounds of tests with statistically relevant samples. Each experiment ran for a minimum of 14 days with at least 2,000 users per variant.

  • H1 confirmed: +9% conversion with progress stepper
  • H2 confirmed: +7% with fixed summary in side panel
  • H3 partially confirmed: +12% on mobile, neutral on desktop
  • H4 not confirmed: social proof had no significant effect

Synthesis of confirmed hypotheses into a redesigned checkout — mobile-first, with stepper, persistent summary and inline card entry. The number of screens was reduced from 5 to 3.

  • Checkout in 3 steps: Delivery → Payment → Confirmation
  • Inline card entry with flip animation when entering CVV
  • Persistent order summary at the top (mobile) or sidebar (desktop)
  • One-click pay for registered users with a saved card

Consolidated results 60 days after launch to 100% of traffic. The project paid back its investment in 11 days of operation.
