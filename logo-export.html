Glass logo — PNG export

Renders the same WebGL logo used in the header, at export resolution, on a transparent background. Pick a size, wait for it to spin to an angle you like, then download and drop the PNG into Figma.

Loading renderer…

Getting it into Figma: drag the downloaded PNG straight onto the canvas, or use Place image (Shift Ctrl K). It arrives with its alpha intact, so it sits on any background.

Why a PNG: Figma has no 3D renderer, so the logo has to be rasterised. Export at 2048 or larger if you plan to scale it up — Figma will downsample cleanly, but it cannot invent detail.
